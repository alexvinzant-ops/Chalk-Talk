      Coach notes, sources & sharing

      Current matchup rule: corner = match; claim a player based on your zone position and stay man for the rest of the possession. The half-court walkthroughs incorporate this newer rule in place of the older guide’s corner-zone or trapping coverage.

      Defensive and five-out diagrams are teaching examples, with example opponent positions. Positions are guides; identify actual threats. Numbers represent roles, not jersey numbers. This edition does not assign players by name.

      Share: use the website link supplied by your coach. Open it in a browser to use playback controls. This web edition loads its animation images from the same website.

      Study sequence: watch once, slow it down, pause before a movement, say your next job, then replay. Use Follow on the drawn diagrams to track one role. Original offensive clips keep their original labels and arrows.
